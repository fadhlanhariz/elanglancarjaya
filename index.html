About Us

Diverse Products, One Trusted Partner

Built On Years Of Experience In Manufacturing Indonesia's Bicycle And Children's Toy Industry, PT Elang Lancar Global Is Dedicated To Supplying Reliable, High-Quality Products To Partners Worldwide. Located At Jalan Kaligawe Raya No. 225, Semarang, Indonesia. Strategically Based In Semarang, Near Major Ports And Airports, We Provide Efficient Logistics, Stable Supply Capacity, And Dependable Global Distribution. We Specialize In Manufacturing Bicycles And Children's Toys That Prioritize Safety, Durability, And Consistent Quality, Ensuring Every Product Meets International Standards And Market Demands. We Understand What Families Need: Products That Are Safe, Durable, Comfortable, And Support A Healthy Lifestyle And Creative Play. Backed By Strong Industry Knowledge And A Commitment To Long-Term Partnerships, We Strive To Be A Trusted Partner For Businesses Seeking Quality Products And Reliable Supply Solutions. Our Mission Is Simple: To Provide Safe, Reliable, And High-Quality Bicycles And Children's Toys, So Customers Can "Ride With Confidence, Play With Purpose." As A Future-Oriented Company, We Continue To Develop Solutions Aligned With Market Needs And Build Sustainable Global Partnerships.

Bike

Our bicycle collection is designed to support active lifestyles, recreation, and everyday mobility. We offer a range of models that combine comfort, durability, functionality, and performance for various riding needs. Whether for casual journeys, outdoor adventures, or daily transportation, each bicycle is built to deliver a smooth and dependable experience. Toys

Our toys are thoughtfully created to inspire imagination, creativity, and joy for children of all ages.
